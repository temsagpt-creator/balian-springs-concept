The club

Guest policies

The atmosphere at Balian Springs is made by everyone in the club. These policies help keep it comfortable and welcoming for all of our guests.

Before you read

How our policies work

We ask every visitor to follow our policies, and they are strictly enforced. Breaking any of them is cause for removal from the club, without refund or compensation.

The policies below explain what we expect of our guests, but they are not the only rules that apply. Balian Springs may change or add policies without advance notice.

Arriving at the club

  • 18 and over

    Guests must be 18 or older to enter, and a government ID is required.

  • Bags

    Bags larger than 10 by 12 inches are not allowed inside, and all bags are subject to search on arrival. We cannot hold belongings that are not allowed in the club.

  • Outside food and drink

    No outside food or drinks are allowed inside. Please finish or throw away any food or drink before you come in. We cannot store food or drinks for you, without exception.

  • Illness

    We may refuse entry to anyone with a contagious illness, a skin condition, or any condition that raises concerns about health code rules and cleanliness, or makes other guests uncomfortable.

  • No re-entry

    There is no re-entry. Once you leave the club, your admission does not cover a return.

Prohibited items

Along with the items above, these are not permitted at any time:

  • Firearms and explosives
  • Chemical weapons, including mace and pepper spray
  • Knives, or any sharp or spiked items
  • Drugs, medical or otherwise
  • Animals
  • Alcohol, and any other outside food and drink
Behavior and etiquette

  • Behavior

    We expect every guest to behave appropriately. Unruly behavior, inappropriate activity, discrimination or profanity may be cause for removal. For the comfort of all guests, no public displays of affection are allowed, including hugging, kissing, touching and sitting on laps. Anyone loitering or making other guests uncomfortable will be asked to leave at once, without a refund.

  • Quiet and care

    Please keep conversations and noise low, especially in the sauna rooms and the sleeping area. Be respectful of the people around you, and clean up after yourself. Put everything you use, such as towels, toiletries, hair dryers and condiments, back in its place so others can enjoy it too.

  • In the pools

    Please don’t jump, splash or act recklessly in the pools.

  • In the locker rooms

    Please avoid anything that could make others uncomfortable, including public displays of affection and staring. Respect the Asian and European bathing traditions the club draws on, and act accordingly.

  • Smoking

    Balian Springs is smoke-free. If you leave to smoke, you will need to pay the daily admission again to come back in.

Phones and photos

Please don’t use your phone in these areas:

  • The saunas
  • The locker rooms
  • The Aqua Lounge
  • Quiet areas

Please be mindful of other guests and their privacy with any photos or video, and stay aware of your surroundings and the people around you.

What to wear

  • Robes

    Proper Balian Springs attire is required at all times. Wear a robe or cover over your swimwear on every floor, except in the pool and bath areas.

  • Swimwear

    No thongs or overly revealing swimwear. Swimwear with heavy decoration, such as beading or strings, is not allowed in the pools.

  • Clothing in the pools

    No cotton t-shirts or pants that are not swimwear are allowed in the pools. If you need to wear a shirt or pants in the pool for religious or medical reasons, please wear swim-grade fabric only, such as a wetsuit, swim shirt or swim pants.

  • Footwear

    No outside footwear is allowed in the club, except in the Fitness Center. Please carry your own athletic shoes there, and wear them on any of the equipment.

  • Long hair

    For your own safety, please tie long hair back in a bun or wear a swimming cap.

Alcohol and drinks

  • A limit of three

    Each guest may place 3 orders of alcoholic drinks in total, with one drink per order and no more than one drink at a time.

  • Intoxication

    Anyone who is intoxicated will be refused entry. For health and safety, anyone under the influence may not use the sauna or steam rooms. We have a no tolerance policy on underage drinking and drunk behavior.

  • Outside alcohol and drugs

    No alcohol may be brought onto the property for any reason. Guests found with outside alcohol or drugs will be asked to leave at once, without a refund.

Health, safety and the facility

  • Signs and cautions

    All guests agree to read the regulations and caution signs around the club, especially by the saunas and pools.

  • Medical advice

    If you are elderly, or have heart disease, diabetes, or high or low blood pressure, please consult a doctor before using the pools.

  • Access to areas

    Balian Springs may close or restrict any area of the club, for any reason, without notice.

  • Care of the club

    Neglect or misuse of any Balian Springs property, from furniture and shared toiletries to equipment, locker keys, towels, robes and rooms, is grounds for removal, and the guest pays for any repair.

  • Wi-Fi

    Our free Wi-Fi is a courtesy to guests. Please don’t use it to download illegal or explicit material. We may withdraw access from any guest.

Lockers, wristbands and belongings

  • Your locker

    Store all your personal items and clothing in your assigned locker, and make sure it is locked before you leave it. Security boxes are available to rent for valuables.

  • Your wristband

    Keep your wristband, which is your locker key, with you at all times. A lost, stolen or damaged key is the responsibility of the guest assigned to it, or others in the group. A lost key carries a $300 penalty fee, paid on top of your balance for the visit.

  • Loaned items

    Please return all bags, robes, towels and slippers to their places as you leave. Bags may be bought at checkout for $10.00.

  • Lost property

    Balian Springs is not responsible for items lost or stolen in the club, in vehicles or in lockers. Items left in the Lost & Found can be collected within 30 days, and remain the owner’s responsibility.

Payments, fees and refunds

  • Paying

    Cards must be physically present, unless you pay with Apple Pay. ID will be checked when needed. The payment methods we accept are listed in our FAQ.

  • Service fee

    All services and therapies carry an automatic 20% service fee. An 18% gratuity applies to all alcoholic drinks. Extra gratuities for exceptional service are at your discretion.

  • Admission

    Once you have checked in, admission is not refunded, even if no treatment reservations are available. Please confirm any reservation before your visit.

  • Your balance

    Purchases are not refunded. Please keep track of your running tab during your visit, and raise any issue straight away.

  • Gift cards

    Gift cards may not be reprinted, copied or resold, and there are no extensions, refunds or exchanges. Balian Springs reserves the right to refuse any gift card without notice or refund.

Treatment reservations

  • Booking

    Reservations are encouraged for all services. Please arrive at the club 1 hour before your appointment, and allow for traffic, weather and anything else that could delay you.

  • Cancellations

    Cancelling or changing an appointment with less than 24 hours’ notice carries a 50% charge, and a missed appointment is charged in full.

  • Arriving late

    If you arrive late, your treatment runs for the time that remains in your booking.
