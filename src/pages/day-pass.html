What your day pass includes

Unlimited access to our modern amenities, for as long as you like during the day:

  • Indoor and outdoor hydrotherapy pools

    With the coed plunge pools on the first floor and the rooftop pool above.

  • Saunas and steam rooms

    Eight themed saunas, from Himalayan salt to far infrared.

  • Lounges

    The therapeutic lounges, the fitness center, the media room and the business center.

  • Wellness workshops

    Most of our wellness workshops are included with admission.

  • Lockers, robe, towel and slippers

    You receive a bag, robe, towel and slippers in the locker room, along with toiletries.

Lilikoi Day Spa treatments, food and drink, retail, premium workshops and our Wellness Enhancers are paid for à la carte, and can be paid for as you leave.

Before you come

Good to know

  • 18 and over

    Guests must be 18 or older, and a government ID is required to enter.

  • No reservation needed

    Admission is on a walk-in basis. If you would like a Lilikoi treatment, we recommend booking it before your visit.

  • Holidays

    US holidays are charged at the weekend rate. Christmas Eve is $130, and we are closed on Christmas Day.

  • No re-entry

    Your day pass covers one visit, from opening to closing.

  • Swimsuits and robes

    Swimsuits are required in the pools, and robes are worn throughout your stay.
