How it works

Most workshops come with admission

Your admission covers the majority of our wellness workshops, and classes are complimentary unless we say otherwise.

  • Premium workshops

    Premium workshops are an additional purchase, and you can pay for them as you leave. They do not include daily admission.

  • For guests of the club

    Workshops are open only to guests of Balian Springs, so plan yours into your visit.

  • What’s on

    To find out which workshops are on during your visit, get in touch with us.

We may photograph workshops for our marketing.

The classes

Learning. Practicing. Growing.

Our workshops are interactive and communal, led by wellness vendors and practitioners from here in Virginia and from around the world.

They range from traditional practices to modern techniques, and some are more experimental. There is one for everyone, however far along you are.
