Before you book

Good to know

  • Open daily, 10:00 am to 9:00 pm

    Lilikoi Day Spa keeps its own hours. The club itself is open from 9:00 am to 9:30 pm.

  • Admission is separate

    Admission to Balian Springs is not included in Lilikoi treatments. Entry to the club is purchased separately.

  • A 20% service charge

    A 20% mandatory surcharge is added to all Lilikoi treatments. Gratuities are optional and are left at your discretion.

  • 18 and over

    Guests must be 18 or older to book a treatment.

  • Book ahead

    Reservations at Lilikoi are encouraged, and are held with a valid credit or debit card. On the day, your treatments are added to your wristband and paid for at checkout.

Your appointment

Arriving, changing and cancelling

When should I arrive?

We suggest arriving at Balian Springs an hour before your appointment, to check in, change into your robe and slippers and enjoy the amenities. Please make your way to Lilikoi Day Spa 15 minutes before your treatment.

As a courtesy to the guests after you, a late arrival receives only the time that remains in the appointment, and the full service fee is charged.

What should I bring and wear?

A robe, slippers, a towel and a day locker are ready for you when you arrive. Bring a bathing suit for the coed areas of the club.

For a treatment, undress to the level you are comfortable with. Undergarments are not necessary, but you are welcome to wear them. Our licensed therapists drape a sheet or towel over you discreetly, so your privacy is always kept. For facials, we provide body wraps to cover you.

How do I change or cancel?

Please make any change or cancellation at least 24 hours ahead for a single service, and 48 hours ahead for multiple treatments. Otherwise, a 50% charge is applied to your credit card.

Cancelling a same-day (walk-in) appointment is also charged at 50%, and a missed appointment is charged in full.

How do I pay?

We don’t take payment for treatments ahead of time. Your treatments are added to your wristband, and you settle everything at checkout.

A 20% mandatory surcharge is added to all à la carte services and packages. Gratuities are optional, and go in full to the people who perform your treatment.

Can I bring my phone?

In consideration of other guests, please turn off your phone before you enter the treatment area.
