Our mission

Renew Bodies. Invigorate Spirits. Awaken Minds.

Balian Springs is a self care social club in Alexandria, Virginia, dedicated to renewal, invigoration and holistic well-being.

About us

Wellness with many sides

We believe true wellness has many sides: the mental, physical, spiritual, emotional and social parts of life.

Our mission is to create a place where you can reconnect with yourself and with others. From the hydrotherapy pools and specialty saunas to our award-winning Lilikoi Day Spa and the relaxation lounges, every part of Balian Springs is designed to support your own way of caring for yourself.

From our founder

A necessity for all

“Self-care shouldn’t be a luxury for some, self-care is a necessity for all.”

Stephanie Chon, Founder

Collective Care

Self care is Collective Care

We think of the club as a third space: a community where you can reconnect with yourself and with others.

We bring together local practitioners, wellness brands and forward-thinking businesses to offer a wide range of practices through our workshops, so you can explore and define what wellness means to you.
