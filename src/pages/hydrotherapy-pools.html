Renew your spirit

Hydrotherapy pools, indoors and on the rooftop

Salus Per Aqua, healing through water. Settle into water jets made to sit, stand and lounge in, then move on to the coed plunge pools or the Hinoki Bath.

First floor

The indoor pool

Palms, a carved stone gate and a pool full of stations. Move from one to the next at your own pace.

  • Blue Touch Dial

    Press the blue dial to start the massage jets.

  • Jacuzzi

    A jacuzzi set into the pool, its jets giving a massaging feel. Bring a drink and settle in.

  • Seated Hydro Jet

    Take a seat and let the water press on your lower back, legs and sides, all at the same time.

  • Magnum Jets

    Water from every direction, all around you.

  • Dream Jets

    An underwater massage aimed at your back, thighs and feet while you lounge.

  • Submerged Seating Area

    Sit back in the water with one of our mocktails from The Dry Well.

  • Wading Pool

    Wade in and take it slowly.

First floor

Coed plunge pools

Plunge pools have been around for thousands of years. Ours are coed, and a good place to go after time in one of our sauna rooms.

The warm, hot and cold plunge pools are in the Aqua Lounge, also on the first floor.

Third floor rooftop

The rooftop pool and Hinoki Bath

Our rooftop hydrotherapy pool sits under the sun and in the open air, on the third floor with The Hideout, our rooftop bar, and its cabanas.

  • Resistance Circulation

    Walk against the current for a workout in the water that works your core and legs.

  • Standing Neck Jets

    Raised jets for a water massage on your neck and shoulders.

  • Hinoki Bath

    Made from Japanese cypress, with the warmth and fragrance of the wood all around you.

The rooftop pool also has the Blue Touch Dial, a jacuzzi, the Seated Hydro Jet, Magnum Jets and Dream Jets.

Before you get in

Good to know

  • No swimming

    Our indoor and outdoor pools are hydrotherapy pools, so swimming is not allowed. Most are 3 to 4 feet deep.

  • Open all year

    The outdoor pools stay open in every season, a lovely place to enjoy different kinds of weather. We close them only for safety, such as during thunderstorms and lightning.

  • Water temperature

    We adapt the pool temperatures to the weather outside. The water is heated most of the year, except in summer.

  • Swimsuits

    Conservative swimsuits are required in the pools, so please bring your own. Outside clothes are not allowed in the water.

Older guests, and anyone with heart disease, diabetes, or high or low blood pressure, should check with a doctor before using the pools.
