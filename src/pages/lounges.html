Renew your mind

Relaxation lounges, between the water and the heat

Our therapeutic lounges are designed to help you unwind, de-stress and find inner peace. There is a fitness center and a media room too.

To rest

Lounges for stillness

  • First floorInfrared Lounge

    Lie back on a lounger under the infrared lamps, in their warmth and red glow.

  • Second floorMeditation Room

    White drapes, floor mats and cushions, for self-guided practice or a sound bath.

  • Second floorAromatherapy Room

    A hideaway scented with aromatherapy, where you can meditate, lounge and practice your breathwork.

  • Immersion iLounge

    A screen that fills the wall takes you around the world, from the beaches of Hawaii to the jungles of Bali.

Second floor

Fitness center and media room

  • Fitness Center

    Treadmills, ellipticals, a bike, free weights and an interactive FORME Life Mirror. Please bring athletic shoes, which are required here.

  • Media Room

    Reclining chaises, with TV shows, movies and sports games playing for quiet enjoyment.

In the lounges
