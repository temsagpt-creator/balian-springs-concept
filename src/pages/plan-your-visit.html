Your first visit

What happens when you arrive

  • 1. Check in

    We ask for a government ID and your phone number at check-in. On later visits, the express check-in kiosks let you check in with your phone number.

  • 2. The shoe locker

    The club is barefoot, so your shoes stay in the shoe locker for the day.

  • 3. Your wristband

    Next you receive your wristband, which you use throughout your stay, starting in the locker room. Treatments and purchases go on it, and you pay as you leave. Please keep it with you: a lost wristband carries a $300 fee.

  • 4. The locker room

    Here you are given a bag, robe, towel and slippers, and toiletries are included. Robes are worn throughout your stay. Please return everything as you leave.

  • 5. Your day

    Then the hydrotherapy pools, sauna rooms and lounges are yours until closing.

What to bring

Pack light

  • A physical photo ID, which you need to enter.
  • A conservative swimsuit for the pools.
  • Athletic shoes, if you would like to use the Fitness Center on the second floor.
  • A small bag: bags larger than 10 by 12 inches are not allowed inside, and bags are subject to search on arrival.

We provide the robe, towel, slippers and toiletries. Free Wi-Fi is available.

House rules

Good to know

  • 18 and over

    Guests must be 18 or older, with a government ID, to enter.

  • No reservation needed

    Admission is on a walk-in basis, first come, first served. If you would like a Lilikoi treatment, we recommend booking it before your visit.

  • No re-entry

    Your day pass covers one visit, from opening to closing.

  • Swimsuits in the pools

    Conservative swimsuits are required in the pools. Other clothing is not allowed in the water.

  • No outside food or drink

    Please finish any food or drink before you come in. You can eat and drink at our eateries and The Dry Well.
