Eateries and bars

Three eateries and two bars

From The Garden Café on the first floor to The Hideout on the rooftop, there is somewhere to eat or drink on each of our three floors.

  • First floorThe Garden Café

    Fresh juices, smoothies and light bites, to start your day or for a break at midday.

  • Second floorKuta Kitchen

    Thoughtfully prepared, hearty entrees: salads, wraps, sliders and more.

  • Second floorNiko-Niko Sushi

    Japanese sushi, from classic sashimi to contemporary rolls. Take a seat between amenities.

  • Third floor rooftopThe Hideout

    Wines and crafted cocktails by the rooftop infinity pool and outdoor cabanas, with views of Alexandria.

  • First floorThe Dry Well

    Adaptogen and zero-proof mocktails for mindful drinking, at our poolside bar.

Before you order

Good to know

  • Paid for à la carte

    Food and drink are not included in admission. If you are with us for the day, you can pay as you leave.

  • Meal tax and service charge

    A 4% Fairfax County meal tax applies to all food and drink. Niko-Niko Sushi, The Dry Well and The Hideout also add an automatic service charge.

  • Vegan, gluten-free and more

    Our menus include vegan, vegetarian, dairy-free, gluten-free, nut-free and keto options.

  • Wine and cocktails

    Alcoholic drinks are served at The Hideout on the rooftop. Each guest may order up to three, one drink at a time.

  • No outside food or drink

    Please leave outside food and drinks at home: they are not allowed in the club.

  • Opening hours

    Each eatery and bar keeps its own hours, which differ from the club’s. Call us on (703) 436-⁠9107 to check before you come.
