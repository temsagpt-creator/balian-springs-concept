The club

Where to spend your day

  • Hydrotherapy pools

    Indoor and rooftop pools set with water jets, coed plunge pools and the Hinoki Bath.

  • Sauna rooms

    Eight themed saunas, from Himalayan salt and loess clay to gold, charcoal and far infrared.

  • Aqua Lounge

    Warm, hot and cold plunge pools, dry and steam saunas and showers.

  • Lounges

    Infrared, meditation and aromatherapy lounges, the Immersion iLounge, a fitness center and a media room.

  • Lilikoi Day Spa

    Facials, body treatments and massage, paid for à la carte. We recommend booking ahead.

  • Eateries and bars

    The Garden Café, Kuta Kitchen, Niko-Niko Sushi, mocktails at The Dry Well and The Hideout on the rooftop.

Admission

What a day pass covers

Your day pass includes unlimited access to our hydrotherapy pools, saunas and lounges, along with lockers, toiletries and most of our wellness workshops.

Lilikoi Day Spa treatments, food and drink, premium workshops and our Wellness Enhancers are paid for à la carte, and can be paid for as you leave.
