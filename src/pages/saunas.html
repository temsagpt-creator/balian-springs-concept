Renew your body

Eight themed sauna rooms

Himalayan salt, loess clay, gold, charcoal, far infrared, colored light, steam and cold. All eight are on the first floor, and all are included with your day pass.

First floor

The eight rooms

  • Far Infrared

    A domed room of silver mosaic, lit by slim lines of light and warmed by far infrared.

  • Gold

    Walls, floor and ceiling laid in gold tile, with white benches to lie on.

  • Himalayan Salt

    Walls of salt brick that glow amber from within.

  • Loess Clay

    A rose-colored room with a small brick hut at its center and a mural of birds and flowers.

  • Charcoal

    Dark walls of stacked charcoal around a curved wooden bench.

  • Steam Box

    Our steam room, warm and humid.

  • Chromotherapy

    A wooden room lit by panels of colored light.

  • Cryotherapy

    The one cold room of the eight: white tile, long benches and cold air.

Hot and cold

After the heat

When you have had enough heat, our coed plunge pools and the indoor hydrotherapy pool are on the same floor as the saunas.

For cold water, there is the cold plunge in the Aqua Lounge, along with warm and hot plunge pools and dry and steam saunas.

In the sauna rooms

Good to know

  • Keep voices low

    Please keep conversation quiet in the sauna rooms.

  • Leave your phone outside

    Please don’t use your phone in the saunas.

  • Read the signs

    Please read the guidance posted by the saunas before you go in.

  • A clear head

    Guests under the influence are not permitted in the sauna or steam rooms.
