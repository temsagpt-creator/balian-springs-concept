Aqua Lounge

Warm, hot and cold plunge pools

Our Aqua Lounge on the first floor brings together warm, hot and cold plunge pools, dry and steam saunas, and showers. Move between the heat and the water, then finish with a quick dip in the cold plunge.

Before you go in

Good to know

  • Separate, like the locker rooms

    The Aqua Lounge and the locker rooms are separated rather than coed.

  • No phones

    Please leave your phone in your locker. Phones are not used in the Aqua Lounge.

  • On the same floor

    Our eight themed sauna rooms and the indoor hydrotherapy pool are on the first floor too.
