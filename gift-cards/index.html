Seasonal gift guide

Gift ideas for everyone on your list

Our holiday gift guide suggests a day at the club to suit each person you are buying for.

  • The Beaute

    For those who want to look as good on the outside as self care makes them feel on the inside. Weekend admission with the Himalayan Salt Sauna, Infrared Lounge and Immersion iLounge, and a build-your-own poke bowl from Niko-Niko Sushi.

  • The Warrior

    For the avid golfer, marathon runner or pick-up basketball player. Weekend admission with the Fitness Center, Polar Cold Plunge, hydrotherapy jets, saunas and Immersion iLounges, and a hearty lunch at Kuta Kitchen.

  • The Spiritualist

    For that friend on a spiritual path. Weekend admission with the Chromotherapy Sauna Room, Meditation Room and Aromatherapy Room, and drinks from The Dry Well.

  • The Socialite

    For the one who’s out every night. Weekend admission with the Loess Clay Sauna and Infrared Sauna, then a shared charcuterie board at Kuta Kitchen or a champagne toast with friends at The Hideout.

  • The Executive

    For the professional with a busy schedule, a day to feel productive while putting wellness first. Weekday admission with a Workspace and the Media Room, an adaptogenic mocktail from The Dry Well and a meal from Niko-Niko Sushi.

Lilikoi Day Spa treatments, food and drink are paid for à la carte.
