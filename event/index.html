Private events

Event spaces at Balian Springs

From corporate presentations and intimate dinners to casting calls, milestones and proposals, our spaces in Alexandria bring people together in pursuit of Collective Care.

Make an inquiry See the spaces

On the rooftop

VIP Cabanas and Pergolas

A cabana suits a celebration with friends or a peaceful break on the rooftop. A pergola is a spacious shaded area for a larger group, for birthdays, anniversaries and wedding parties.

  • VIP Cabanas, up to 4 guests

    $275

  • VIP Pergolas, 8 to 10 guests

    $600

Each 2-hour rental includes your choice of lemonade, house sangria, house champagne or infused water, and a complimentary seasonal essentials kit for your guests. A pergola also comes with a complimentary fresh fruit platter.

Your own attendant takes your food and drink orders, and an 18% service charge is added.

Inside the club

Rooms for your group

  • Tropical Atrium

    Up to 80 guests. An open space to set up as you like.

  • Immersion iLounge

    Up to 60 guests. 16 lounge chairs, use of the sound system, and your choice of view from 10 custom backgrounds.

  • Sleeping Area

    Up to 40 guests. 24 full-body lounge mats.

  • Work Space

    Up to 25 guests. Tables and seating for 15, a TV and a whiteboard.

  • Media Room

    Up to 24 guests. 24 theater style recliners, 4 TVs and 1 projection screen, with a versatile media player and a sound system.

  • Aqua Lounge

    Up to 20 guests. A private steam sauna and dry sauna, private showers, and private warm, hot and cold baths.

  • Event Space

    Up to 20 guests. A table and seating for 15.

  • Meditation Room

    Up to 12 guests. Meditation mats.

Catering

Food for your group

Catering is served buffet style, with water and lemonade included. Prices are per person, everyone in the group chooses the same option, and an 18% service charge is added per person.

  • Up to 25 guests: $32 per person

    One appetizer (side salad or fruit) and one entrée (Animal Style Sliders or Fried Green Tomatoes, with fries).

  • Up to 25 guests: $42 per person

    One appetizer (charcuterie board or ceviche) and one entrée (Animal Style Sliders or Fried Green Tomatoes, with fries).

  • 26 or more guests: $35 per person

    Fruit, a charcuterie board and grilled chicken skewers, or fruit, chips and salsa with guacamole, and chicken tacos.

  • 26 or more guests: $45 per person

    Fruit, ceviche and chicken tacos, or ceviche, a California roll and a Niko-Niko roll.

  • Rooftop Grill, 26 or more guests: $45 per person

    Fruit, charcuterie, pasta salad, chicken skewers, and sausage with peppers and onions.

Drinks

Bar service and add-ons

Our outdoor or exclusive bar service is priced per person. It includes a 20% service charge and allows for our 3-drink maximum.

  • Bar service: $60 per person

    House wine, with 2 cocktails and 1 mocktail of your choice.

  • Bar service: $80 per person

    House wine, house beer and house champagne, with 2 cocktails and 1 mocktail of your choice.

Choose your cocktails from the Balian Breeze, Cucumber Cooler, Golden Sunrise and The Blue Monarch, and your mocktail from the Social Butterfly, Zen Spritz and The Royal Orchid.

  • Drinks add-on: $50 per pitcher or bottle

    White wine sangria, with or without alcohol, or a house bottle of red, white or rosé wine. Each serves 4 to 6 people, with no more than 1 bottle or pitcher per 4 guests in your group.

  • Dessert add-on: $35 per tray

    Cookies or cupcakes, one or the other. Each tray serves 4 to 6.

Beer, liquor and CBD are served separately at the bar.

Good to know

Before you plan

  • Celebrations

    Outside food and drink, cakes included, can’t be brought in. We do offer a celebration package that you can buy when you arrive, for celebrations at the club.

  • 18 and over

    Every guest must be 18 or older, and a government ID is required to enter.
