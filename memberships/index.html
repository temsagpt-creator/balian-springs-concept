Sunset

Your evenings at the club

For when your time is limited, but you still want to make room for self care after hours.

  • Unlimited evening entry

    From 5:00 pm to 9:30 pm, every day, except on private event days.

  • Full day add-on

    Come for the full day for $75 on weekdays, or $110 on weekends and holidays.

  • Retail

    10% off select retail products.

All Access

Every day, opening to closing

For self care enthusiasts who want to share their practice with friends and family.

  • Unlimited entry

    From 9:00 am to 9:30 pm, every day, except on private event days.

  • Guest passes

    Depending on your plan, bring 2 guests per day ($95 on weekdays, $130 on weekends and holidays) or 4 guests per day ($95 on weekdays, $110 on weekends and holidays).

  • Retail

    10% or 20% off select retail products, depending on your plan.
