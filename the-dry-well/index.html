The drinks

Mocktails for mindful drinking

We make thoughtful, tasteful non-alcoholic drinks for mindful drinking. At the bar you can order:

  • Adaptogen mocktails
  • CBD (cannabidiol) mocktails and drinks
  • Zero-proof mocktails and other non-alcoholic drinks

Our private event bar packages include a mocktail that your group chooses from three:

  • Social Butterfly
  • Zen Spritz

Where to find it

By the pool, on the first floor

The Dry Well sits on the first floor, with the indoor hydrotherapy pool, the sauna rooms and The Garden Café.

Take your mocktail into the indoor pool, where the submerged seating area is made for sitting back and sipping.

We welcome guests from across Northern Virginia and Washington, DC, at 6432 General Green Way, Alexandria, VA 22312.

Before you order

Good to know

  • Paid for à la carte

    Drinks at The Dry Well are not included in admission. If you are with us for the day, you can pay as you leave.

  • Meal tax and service charge

    A 4% Fairfax County meal tax and an automatic service charge apply to all purchases at The Dry Well.

  • Opening hours

    The Dry Well keeps its own hours, which differ from the club’s. Call us on (703) 436-⁠9107 to check before you come.

  • 18 and over

    Guests must be 18 or older, and a government ID is required to enter the club.
