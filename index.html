Renew your body

Eight saunas, each with its own element.

Featuring heat, cold temperatures, infrared rays and natural essences. All eight are on the first floor, steps from the pools.

  1. Far Infrared

    Heaters convert infrared light into heat that is absorbed into the skin.

  2. Gold

    Used from the Ancient Romans to the Egyptians to beautify and to heal.

  3. Himalayan Salt

    Humidity condenses on the salt blocks, triggering a natural ionization.

  4. Loess Clay

    Mineral-rich loess soil from South Korea, compressed into clay.

  5. Charcoal

    Activated charcoal, known for its porous and absorbent qualities.

  6. Steam Box

    Superheated sauna rocks create extra humidity.

  7. Chromotherapy

    Color therapy, believed to help balance the energy within oneself.

  8. Cryotherapy

    Short-term cold stimulation, and a refreshing escape from the heat.

Plan your visit

Stay at least half a day.

Bring your bathing suit for our co-ed amenity spaces. Everything else is waiting for you.

  1. Check in

    Bring a government-issued ID. You’ll receive a numbered electronic wristband to use throughout your visit.

  2. Settle in

    In the locker room you’ll be provided with a bag, robe, towel, and slippers.

  3. Explore

    Pools, saunas, lounges and workshops, at your own pace, from open to close.

  4. Check out

    Spa services, food, and drinks on your wristband are settled as you leave.
