How it works

  • Walk in or call

    K Body Scrubs are offered by walk-in or phone appointment only. Call us at (703) 436-9107.

  • In our Aqua Lounge

    Scrubs take place in our Aqua Lounge, from 10:00 am to 6:00 pm. Please call to check which days they are offered.

  • A same-gender therapist

    This is a fully nude service, performed by a therapist of the same gender.

  • A 20% service charge

    A 20% service charge is added to all scrubs and enhancements.

Before you book

Good to know

  • Admission is separate

    Admission to Balian Springs is not included in treatments. Entry to the club is purchased separately.

  • Service charge and gratuities

    A 20% mandatory surcharge is added to all Lilikoi treatments. Gratuities are optional and are left at your discretion.

  • 18 and over

    Guests must be 18 or older to book a treatment.

  • Arriving and cancelling

    Arrival times and cancellation charges are set out on the Lilikoi Day Spa page.
